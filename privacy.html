Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026. is a service of Ghost Systems LLC ("we"). It tracks company trucks for the business that owns them. This page says what we collect, what we never collect, who can see it and how to get it out or delete it.

The short version

  • We track trucks, not people. Location comes from a tracker plugged into the truck.
  • A phone's location is read only at the moment someone taps a button that needs it. Never in the background.
  • No microphone and no camera facing the driver. Not in any version.
  • We don't sell data and we don't show ads.
  • A connected calendar is read-only. We never write to it.
  • The records belong to the business. They can be downloaded at any time and deleted on request.

What we collect

From the tracker in a truck: where the truck is, its speed and direction, whether the engine is on, battery voltage, the odometer, engine hours and engine fault codes, and whether the tracker was unplugged. From these we work out trips, stops, time at each job and mileage.

From a phone, only on a tap: the phone's location at the moment someone taps "I'm there now", looks up an address, or clocks in at a job (if the business has turned the time clock on). The app has no background location.

Account details: each person's name and mobile number (the number is how they sign in), their role, their language, and a notification token for their phone. For the business: its name, time zone, working hours, billing email and the address trackers are shipped to.

What the business puts in: trucks, jobs, customer names, addresses and phone numbers, notes, saved places, who is in which truck, service records, papers with dates, fuel receipts, what was done on a job, and photos (receipts, papers, and before and after photos of a job).

Payments: handled by Stripe on this website. We never see or store a card number.

Website: no advertising or tracking cookies. The pages keep your sign-in and language choice in your browser.

Google Calendar

The owner of a business can choose to connect a Google Calendar so jobs don't have to be typed twice. If they do:

  • We ask Google for two read-only permissions: to see the list of calendars, and to see events. We never ask to create, change or delete anything.
  • We read events only from the calendars the owner ticks, from today to 14 days ahead.
  • From an event that has a location we keep its title, location, description, day and start time, and offer it to the owner as a job to add. Events with no location are not kept.
  • A key that lets our server keep reading is stored where the app and other customers cannot reach it. Disconnecting in the app deletes that key and anything still waiting, and tells Google to cancel it.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar data is used only to show the business its own upcoming jobs. It is not used for advertising, is not sold, is not used to train AI models, and is not read by our staff except when the business asks us for help, when needed for security, or when the law requires it.

What we never collect

  • Sound. The tracker and the app have no microphone access.
  • Video or photos of the driver.
  • A phone's location in the background.
  • Contacts, messages or anything else on a phone. Texts the app helps write are sent from the person's own Messages app; we don't see them.
  • Driver scores or rankings.

Who can see it

Inside the business: the owner and dispatchers see the trucks, trips, jobs and records. Crew see the jobs sent to them and their own records. The app has a screen called "What my boss sees" that shows each crew member exactly what is tracked under their company's settings, including every time a truck with off-hours privacy was looked up.

Off-hours privacy: the owner can switch it on for a truck that goes home with its driver. Outside working hours that truck is off the map and its trips are not shown. A look-up is possible, is limited in time, needs a reason and is logged where the crew can see it.

Links the business sends: a job link, an "on my way" link or a proof-of-service link shows only that one job to whoever has the link, and stops working when it expires.

Other businesses: never. Each company's data is walled off from every other company's.

Companies that help us run the service and handle data only to do that: Supabase (database and file storage), Stripe (payments), Twilio (sign-in text messages), Apple, Google and Expo (delivering notifications), Mapbox, Apple and Google (maps and address look-up), and Anthropic (reading the totals off a fuel receipt photo when someone scans one).

We may disclose information when the law requires it. We don't sell or rent it to anyone.

How long we keep it

For as long as the business is a customer. After a business cancels, its owner can still sign in on this website and download its records for 12 months; after that we may delete them. Anyone can delete their own account from Settings in the app. When an owner deletes the account, the whole company's data is deleted.

Your choices

  • Download: the owner can export trips, mileage, job visits, service and fuel from the dashboard on this website.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete account in the app, or ask us.
  • Disconnect a calendar: Company → Calendar in the app.
  • If you drive a tracked truck: your employer decides whether and how company vehicles are tracked. Ask them first; we will help them answer.

Children

The service is for businesses and the adults who work for them. It is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app or by email to the owner before it takes effect.

Contact

Ghost Systems LLC. Use "Get help" in the app's Settings.
