The terms, in plain words

  • Month to month. No contract. Cancel any time from your dashboard. No phone call, no notice period.
  • Your price is locked. The per-truck price you sign up at stays the same for as long as you stay subscribed.
  • 30-day refund. Send the tracker back in your first 30 days and we refund your first month and the setup fee in full. After that, no refunds for part months.
  • Pause for the season. Park a truck for the winter or the summer and pause it for up to 6 months a year. A paused truck is free, and still alerts you if it moves.
  • The tracker is yours to keep. If you cancel, we switch off its connection. Nothing to send back.
  • Your records stay yours. Download trips, mileage and job visits any time, and for 12 months after you cancel.
